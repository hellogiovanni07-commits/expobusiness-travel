05

Voice & messaging

Direct, specific and calm, like a good coordinator at the hall entrance. Name the fair, the phase, the city and the date. Never hype.

China’s trade expos, fully managed for your businessBuy direct. We handle the trip.From Manila to the hall floorOne phase, one quotationYour coordinator in Guangzhou
BOILERPLATE · 25 WORDS

ExpoBusiness Travel Services plans business trips from the Philippines to China’s trade expos, led by the Canton Fair: flights, hotels, buyer badges, visas and interpreters.

BOILERPLATE · 60 WORDS

ExpoBusiness Travel Services is a B2B travel and MICE agency for Philippine companies buying from China. We build trips around the Canton Fair’s three phases and China’s major expos in Guangzhou, Shanghai, Hong Kong, Shenzhen and beyond. One itemised quotation covers flights, hotels near the venue, buyer registration, M visa assistance, interpreters, factory visits and group delegations.

We say

  • “Phase 1, Apr 15–19, Pazhou”
  • “Itemised quotation”
  • “Your coordinator meets you at arrivals”
  • “Expected dates, confirmed before booking”

We avoid

  • “Unforgettable journey”, “seamless”, “world-class”
  • Exclamation marks and ALL CAPS hype
  • Promising dates the organiser hasn’t published
  • Leisure-tour language
