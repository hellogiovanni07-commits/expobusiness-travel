Company description

ExpoBusiness Travel Services plans business travel for Philippine companies from Metro Manila: corporate trips, trade shows, trade missions and delegations, with one coordinator per trip.

ExpoBusiness Travel Services is a Metro Manila business travel company. We plan corporate travel, trade-show and expo trips, trade missions, delegations and MICE for Philippine companies travelling worldwide. One coordinator handles visas, flights, hotels, registration and ground transport, and nothing is booked or paid until you approve the itemised proposal.

ExpoBusiness Travel Services plans business travel for Philippine companies from Metro Manila, in partnership with Immaculate Connections. We organise corporate travel for HR, admin and finance teams; trade-show and expo trips for buyers and exhibitors; trade missions for importers and sourcing teams; and delegations and MICE for chambers, associations and LGUs. Our 2027 calendar lists 102 trade expos in 21 countries, from China and Japan to Europe, the Middle East and the USA. Each trip has one named coordinator, an itemised proposal, and official invoices and receipts in your company’s name. Nothing is booked or paid until you approve it.

IV

Voice

Plain British English for the people who sign off travel budgets. Corporate, confident, short sentences.

  • Plain.The word an HR officer would use, not a brochure word.
  • Exact.Dates, headcounts, cities. Numbers over adjectives.
  • Accountable.Say what we do and who does it. One coordinator.
  • Honest.Expected is labelled expected. Nothing we cannot prove.

        Accessibility rules

        • Contrast4.5 : 1 for body text, 3 : 1 for 24 px+ or 19 px bold. The matrix in chapter VII is the check.
        • Text sizeBody 16 px; never below 14 px on screen or 8 pt in print. Pages zoom to 200% without loss.
        • FocusA visible 2 px orange ring on every link, button and field. Never remove outlines.
        • Targets44 × 44 px minimum, 8 px between neighbouring targets.
        • Colour aloneNever the only signal: errors get words, charts get labels, do and don’t get ticks and crosses.
        • ImagesAlt text that says what the image shows; decorative images get empty alt.
        • LinksSay where they go (“Browse the 2027 calendar”), never “click here”.
        • FormsVisible labels, errors under the field in plain words, no placeholder-only fields.
        • MotionRespect reduced motion; nothing flashes; loops pause; video has captions.
        • Languagelang="en-GB" on every page; PDFs tagged where the tool allows.
