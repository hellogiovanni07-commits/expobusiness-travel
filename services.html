Corporate services

Turnkey business travel, from one accountable team

Every piece of the trip, booked, documented and tracked by a coordinator in Cebu.

01 · Corporate travel

Flights and hotels, booked to your policy

For companies that send people abroad for meetings, audits, training or sales calls.

  • Fares compared across airlines, with baggage and change rules spelled out
  • Business hotels close to where your team actually works
  • Date changes, name changes and rebooking handled for you
  • Departures from Cebu, Manila, Clark and Davao

02 · Trade missions & expos

Trade show trips that end in orders

For buyers and procurement teams attending trade expos in Asia, Europe, the Middle East and the US.

  • Buyer or visitor registration and invitation letters
  • Hotels near the venue, booked early for fair weeks
  • Hall planning by product category
  • Supplier meetings and factory visits around fair days
See the 2027 expo calendar

03 · Delegations & MICE

Delegations that move as one programme

For chambers of commerce, associations and companies running incentive trips, conferences or group missions.

  • Group airfare and seat blocks; charter flights on request
  • Rooming lists, coaches and a delegation lead on the ground
  • Group visa filing and a pre-departure briefing
  • Meeting venues, business dinners and gala events

04 · Interpreters & ground logistics

On the ground, nothing left to chance

The details that decide whether a business trip runs on time.

  • Bilingual interpreters by the half or full day
  • Airport transfers, private vans and drivers
  • Factory visit routing and transport
  • Introductions to freight forwarders for samples and cargo
