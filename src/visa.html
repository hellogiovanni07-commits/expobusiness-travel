Visa requirements at a glance

Ordinary Philippine passports, short business visits. Rules change; we confirm each case in your proposal.

Destination example: China business (M) visa

Required for trade fairs such as the Canton Fair, supplier meetings and factory visits in mainland China. Other destinations follow a similar checklist; we send yours with the proposal.

  1. 01

    Passport valid for at least six months, with blank visa pages

  2. 02

    Online application completed and approved before the visit to the visa centre

  3. 03

    Recent passport photo to the required specification

  4. 04

    Invitation letter from a Chinese company or the trade fair organiser

  5. 05

    Proof of your business, such as DTI or SEC registration
