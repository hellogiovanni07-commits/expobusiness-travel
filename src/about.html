Before you book

Who is ExpoBusiness?

ExpoBusiness Travel Services plans business travel for Philippine companies. We are based in Metro Manila and work in partnership with Immaculate Connections, a Philippine travel and tour services company.

Do you issue official receipts?

Yes. Invoices and official receipts are issued in your company's name.

Can you arrange departures outside Manila?

Yes. We plan trips from Manila and can also arrange departures from Cebu, Clark and Davao.

Is payment taken on the website?

No. You receive a written proposal first. Payment terms are agreed before anything is booked.
