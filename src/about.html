Before you book

Who is behind ExpoBusiness?

ExpoBusiness Travel Services is a subsidiary of Immaculate Connections, a travel company based in Cebu City, Philippines.

Do you issue official receipts?

Yes. Invoices and official receipts are issued in your company's name.

Can you book travel from outside Cebu?

Yes. We plan departures from Cebu, Manila, Clark and Davao and work with companies anywhere in the Philippines.

Is payment taken on the website?

No. Nothing is paid on the website. You receive a written proposal first, and payment terms are agreed before anything is booked.
