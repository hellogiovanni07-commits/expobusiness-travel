Buyer checklist

Questions our coordinators help you ask at the booth and the factory.

  • Check fabric composition and weight on the sample
  • Ask for size charts and grading
  • Confirm MOQ per style, colour and size
  • Request a pre-production sample in your fabric
  • Agree labelling and care-label requirements
  • Plan inspection for measurements and finishing

Textile labelling rules apply in the Philippines. Agree fibre content and care labels with your supplier before production.
